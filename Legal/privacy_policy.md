# Privacy Policy for QuickLaunch

**Last updated: September 18, 2024**

## Overview

QuickLaunch ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our QuickLaunch application.

## Information We Collect

### Information We DO NOT Collect

QuickLaunch is designed with privacy as a core principle. We **do not collect, store, or transmit** any of the following:

- Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Application usage statistics or analytics
- User behavior data
- Network or internet activity
- Device identifiers or hardware information
- Location data
- Any data that could identify you personally

### Information Processed Locally

QuickLaunch processes the following information **locally on your device only**:

- **Application Metadata**: Names, icons, and file paths of applications installed on your Mac
- **User Preferences**: Your custom app arrangements, folder organizations, and display settings
- **Application Cache**: Temporary storage of app information to improve performance

All this information:
- Remains on your device at all times
- Is never transmitted over the network
- Is not accessible to us or any third parties
- Is stored using standard macOS security practices

## How We Use Your Information

Since we don't collect any personal information, we cannot use your data for any purpose. All data processing happens locally on your device to:

- Display your installed applications
- Remember your custom arrangements
- Improve app launch performance through caching
- Provide search functionality

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage Only
- All data is stored locally using macOS standard APIs (UserDefaults, Keychain when appropriate)
- No data is stored on external servers or cloud services
- Data is protected by your macOS user account permissions

### App Sandbox Security
QuickLaunch runs in Apple's App Sandbox, which provides additional security by:
- Limiting access to only necessary system resources
- Preventing unauthorized access to your personal files
- Ensuring compliance with macOS security standards

## Third-Party Services

QuickLaunch **does not use any third-party services**, including:
- No analytics services (Google Analytics, etc.)
- No crash reporting services
- No advertising networks
- No social media integrations
- No external APIs or web services

## Children's Privacy

QuickLaunch does not collect any information from users of any age. The app is suitable for all ages as it does not process any personal information.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Since QuickLaunch doesn't collect personal information, changes to this policy will primarily be for clarity or compliance purposes.

## Data Rights

Since we don't collect personal information, traditional data rights (access, deletion, etc.) don't apply. However, you have complete control over your data because:

- All data remains on your device
- You can delete the app at any time to remove all associated data
- You can reset app preferences through the app's settings

## Compliance

This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with:
- Apple App Store Review Guidelines
- macOS App Sandbox requirements
- General privacy best practices

## Technical Implementation

For transparency, here's how we ensure your privacy:

### No Network Access
- QuickLaunch does not request network permissions
- The app cannot connect to the internet
- All operations are performed locally

### Minimal Permissions
QuickLaunch only requests the minimum permissions necessary:
- **File System Access**: Limited to reading application directories (/Applications, ~/Applications, etc.)
- **Apple Events**: Only to launch applications you select

### No Telemetry
- No usage statistics are collected
- No crash reports are sent
- No performance metrics are gathered
- No user behavior is tracked

## Summary

QuickLaunch is a privacy-first application launcher that:
- ✅ Processes all data locally on your device
- ✅ Never transmits any information over the network
- ✅ Doesn't collect personal information
- ✅ Operates within Apple's security sandbox
- ✅ Gives you complete control over your data

Your privacy is not just protected by policy—it's protected by design.
