# Privacy Policy for QuickLaunch

**Last updated: December 2024**

## Introduction

QuickLaunch ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our QuickLaunch application for macOS.

## Information We DO NOT Collect

QuickLaunch is designed with privacy as a core principle. We want to be completely transparent about what we do and do not collect:

### We DO NOT collect:
- Personal information (names, email addresses, phone numbers)
- Usage analytics or telemetry data
- Application usage patterns or statistics
- Crash reports or diagnostic data (unless explicitly sent by you)
- Any data about which applications you have installed or use
- Network activity or browsing data
- Location information
- Device identifiers or hardware information

## Information Stored Locally

QuickLaunch stores the following information locally on your Mac only:

### Application Cache
- A list of applications found on your system (stored in your user preferences)
- Application icons (cached locally for performance)
- Your custom app arrangement and positioning

### User Preferences
- Your preferred app layout and organization
- Window position and size preferences
- Search history (stored locally, never transmitted)

### Crash Logs
- If the application encounters an error, crash logs may be saved locally
- These logs are stored in your Application Support folder
- Crash logs are never automatically sent to us
- You have full control over whether to share crash logs for support purposes

## How Information Is Used

All information stored by QuickLaunch is used solely to:
- Provide faster app launching through caching
- Remember your preferred app organization
- Maintain your personalized experience across app launches

## Data Storage and Security

- All data is stored locally on your Mac using standard macOS security practices
- We use encrypted user preferences storage provided by macOS
- No data is transmitted to external servers
- No network connections are made for data collection purposes

## Third-Party Services

QuickLaunch does not integrate with any third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or data collection services.

## Children's Privacy

QuickLaunch does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 years of age.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

## Data Deletion

Since QuickLaunch only stores data locally on your Mac, you can delete all application data by:
1. Uninstalling the QuickLaunch application
2. Removing the application preferences (optional)
3. Clearing the application cache from your Library folder
